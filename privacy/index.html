Privacy Policy, Kiyara

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Kiyara is an internal assistant service operated by Advanced Food Co. ("AFC"), Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. It is used only by authorised AFC staff to receive work notifications and to approve business communications through WhatsApp and email.

What we collect

  • The WhatsApp phone number of the authorised staff member who uses the service.
  • The content of messages and files exchanged with the Kiyara WhatsApp number, including text, documents and images.
  • Basic technical data provided by the WhatsApp Business Platform, such as message IDs, timestamps and delivery status.

How we use it

  • To send work notifications, draft documents and approval requests to authorised staff.
  • To receive and act on the replies of authorised staff, such as approving or editing a draft.
  • We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell it.

Who can use the service

Kiyara accepts messages only from phone numbers authorised by AFC. Messages from any other number are not processed and are discarded.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the service: Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), our email provider, and the AI and hosting tools AFC uses internally. Each processes the data only to provide its service. We do not share information with any other third party unless required by law.

Storage and security

Messages and files are stored on AFC-controlled systems with restricted access. Access keys are stored securely and are never shared. Incoming messages are verified as genuinely sent by WhatsApp before they are processed.

Retention

Messages are kept for as long as needed for the related business task and are deleted when no longer required, or on request.

Your rights

Authorised users may ask to see, correct or delete their data, or stop using the service at any time.
